Flowtide Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Flowtide is a connect-the-dots and pipes puzzle game published by Mirza Games. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Gameplay data on your device only. Level progress, completed boards, streaks, missions, coins and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not run accounts and do not upload your saves. If you use Google Play Games cloud save, Google stores that copy under its own policy.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, for example which levels players reach and where they stop. This includes an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, country derived from IP address, and in-game events. It does not include your name or contact details.
  • Crash reports. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs and device state when the app crashes, so we can fix bugs.
  • Advertising. Ads are served through AppLovin MAX and its mediation partners (for example AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource, Mintegral, Vungle and Meta Audience Network). They may use the advertising ID of your device to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Purchases. Optional purchases (Remove ads, coin packs) are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase token and product ID, never your payment details.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, precise location, or any content you create. The game does not require an account.

Children

Flowtide is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

  • AppLovin (MAX): applovin.com/privacy; mediation partners are listed in Settings > Privacy options inside the app
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Crashlytics: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing and Google Play Games: policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices

  • Ads: manage consent in Settings > Privacy options (where applicable) or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Analytics: you can opt out of Google Analytics collection by contacting us.
  • Data deletion: local data is removed when you uninstall the app. To request deletion of analytics data tied to your app instance, contact us with your device details.

How to request deletion of your data

Flowtide (published by Mirza Games) does not use accounts, so most of your data never leaves your device. To delete it:

  1. Game progress, completed boards, coins and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling Flowtide deletes them.
  2. Analytics and crash data (app-instance ID, device model, OS version, in-game events, crash logs) are held by Google Analytics for Firebase and Crashlytics. To have them deleted, email sezarr.com@gmail.com with the subject "Flowtide data deletion request" and your device model. We delete the analytics data linked to your app instance within 30 days and confirm by email.
  3. Advertising identifiers are controlled by Android. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

We keep nothing else. Purchase records are held by Google Play under its own policy.

Contact

Email: sezarr.com@gmail.com

Changes
