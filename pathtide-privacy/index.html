Pathtide Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Pathtide is a connect-the-dots and pipes puzzle game published by Mirza Games. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Gameplay data on your device. Dots and Pipes level progress and stars, completed boards, streaks and records, daily puzzle results, missions, coins and settings are stored on your device. The game has no accounts of its own.
  • Google Play Games (optional). On first launch the game offers to sign you in to Google Play Games. You can decline and still play everything, and sign in later from the leaderboard or achievements buttons. If you sign in, Google Play Games uses your player ID, gamertag and avatar, receives your scores for the leaderboards and the achievements you unlock, and keeps a cloud copy of your game progress (Saved Games: Dots and Pipes levels and stars, completed boards, records, daily puzzle results, coins, settings, streaks and achievements), so your progress comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone. Leaderboards show your gamertag, avatar and scores to other players, depending on your Play Games profile settings. Google stores this data under its own privacy policy.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, for example which levels players reach and where they stop. It collects an app-instance identifier, your device's advertising ID, device model, operating system version, your approximate location (such as the country or city, derived from your IP address), in-game events (such as Dots and Pipes boards solved, with their size, time and hints, and hints used) and purchase events. It does not include your name or contact details.
  • Crash reports. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs (stack traces), the state of your device when the app crashed and a Crashlytics installation identifier, so we can fix bugs.
  • Game settings. Firebase Remote Config uses a Firebase installation ID, together with the app version and your device's language, country setting and time zone, to download settings for the game, such as coin prices, without an app update.
  • Purchases. Optional purchases (coin packs, plus Remove ads when ads are available) are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or other payment details. The app receives the product ID and a purchase token from Google Play to deliver your item, and Google Analytics for Firebase records the purchase (product, price and currency).
  • Advertising. Pathtide includes the AppLovin MAX advertising SDK, but ads are switched on only by an app update. Until then, as in the current version, the SDK is never started and no ads are shown. Once ads are switched on, AppLovin and its advertising partners may use your device's advertising ID, device information (such as model, operating system and network type), your ad interactions (such as ads viewed or tapped) and your approximate location, derived from your IP address, to show ads, measure them and prevent ad fraud. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app then asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and Settings shows a Privacy options entry where you can change your choice at any time.
  • Daily reminder (optional). If you turn on the daily reminder in Settings, the notification is scheduled on your device. Nothing about it is sent to us or anyone else.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your real name, email address or phone number, and we do not collect your contacts, photos, files, messages or precise (GPS) location. The only name involved is your Google Play Games gamertag, and only if you sign in. The game does not require an account.

Children

Pathtide is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

  • Google Play Games (sign-in, leaderboards, achievements and Saved Games) and Google Play Billing: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • AppLovin (MAX), once ads are switched on: legal.applovin.com/privacy; the advertising partners in use are then listed in the consent form where the app shows one

Your choices

  • Google Play Games: signing in is optional. You can delete your Play Games profile and its games data, including saved games, through your Play Games profile or your Google Account.
  • Ads: once ads are switched on, manage your consent in Settings > Privacy options where it is shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings at any time.
  • Daily reminder: off until you turn it on. Turn it off again in the game's Settings or in Android's notification settings.
  • Analytics and crash reports: these run in every copy of the game and cannot be turned off in the app. You can ask us to delete the data linked to your app instance, as described below.

How to request deletion of your data

Pathtide (published by Mirza Games) has no accounts of its own, and most of your data never leaves your device. To delete it:

  1. Game progress, boards, coins and settings on your device: uninstalling Pathtide deletes them.
  2. Google Play Games data (your player profile, leaderboard scores, achievements and the Saved Games cloud copy) is held by Google. You can delete it through your Play Games profile or your Google Account.
  3. Analytics and crash data (app-instance ID, installation IDs, device model, OS version, approximate location, in-game and purchase events, crash logs) are held by Google Analytics for Firebase and Crashlytics. To have them deleted, email sezarr.com@gmail.com with the subject "Pathtide data deletion request" and your device model. We delete the analytics data linked to your app instance within 30 days and confirm by email.
  4. Advertising identifiers are controlled by Android. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads. Once ads are switched on, AppLovin handles requests about the advertising data it holds under its own privacy policy.

We keep nothing else. Purchase records are held by Google Play under its own policy.
